Your price isn’t the problem it’s the filter. The right people see your value, the rest move on.

Someone asked how much our drinks was, and we answered ₱79. One person said it was too pricey but another went ahead and ordered 3 Latte. Later, someone else asked the same question. When we said ₱79, they thought it was expensive. But another customer reminded them that if she bought it somewhere else, it would cost over ₱200. What seems expensive to one person may be reasonable to another. The right people don’t complain about the price, they recognize the value. Your price is not the problem. Your price is the FILTER. It weeds out those who aren’t ready and attracts those who truly appreciate your worth. Remember this: you are not meant for everyone. You are meant for the people who see the value you bring. To every business owner, may you always attract the right customers who honor your effort, respect your craft, and value your hard work. 🙏 #CalleCo
